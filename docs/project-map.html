NAMI Dashboard Suite: the plan on one page

For NAMI Southeast Wisconsin leadership and Jesse Berg. Dated October 5, 2026. Three programs today, built to take more.

Week 1: Volunteers & Events starts
Week 2: Dashboard starts, on practice grants
Week 3: real grants; Radar any time
Weeks 2 and 4: check-ins
After week 4: decision
Weeks 5–7: NAMI's own home

Weeks count from the day the first email goes out. One program starts at a time, each with its own owner. Phase two waits for one decision and one named person, so its dates move with those.

Phase one · now · testing

Try the programs on the links we have today

Nothing new to set up. Each owner opens the landing page and starts, one program at a time. The goal is honest feedback: what is confusing, slow or missing.

Steps

  1. Jesse turns on the landing page, brings the Grant Dashboard up to date, and invites the volunteer coordinator to Volunteers & Events.
  2. The Executive Director forwards each program's email to its owner, Jesse copied: Volunteers & Events in week 1, the Grant Dashboard in week 2, Grant Radar any time.
  3. Each owner has a 20-minute walkthrough with Jesse in their first week, by phone, screen share or in person, entering one real thing together.
  4. Owners use the programs for real, small things. The Grant Dashboard stays on practice grants for two weeks, then takes real grants with a weekly backup.
  5. Owners keep a 15-minute weekly routine: open it, mark what happened, save a backup.
  6. Jesse emails three questions at weeks 2 and 4, and fixes as feedback comes in. Changes reach the links within minutes.
  7. The Executive Director confirms the internal person by week 4 (proposed: the office manager or the volunteer coordinator).

Where things live in phase one

  • The code: Jesse's GitHub, open to read, with the guides.
  • The running programs: the Grant Dashboard on its current site; Grant Radar on the landing page, no sign-in, each person's own list; Volunteers & Events on a shared page with a Claude sign-in, which is what keeps the volunteer list private.
  • The rule for all three: no health details, background-check reports, ID numbers or bank details. Money gifts belong in NAMI's donor system; Volunteers & Events keeps only money given for an event.
Phase two · after the decision · NAMI's own home

Move the programs into a place NAMI owns, with one sign-in

Same programs, same guides. What changes is who owns the accounts, where the records live, and how people get in: one sign-in for all three, by a code sent to the person's NAMI email, with Microsoft sign-in possible later.

Steps

  1. NAMI creates a GitHub organization and a Cloudflare account (both free at this size), with two owners: the internal person and Jesse.
  2. Jesse moves the code into the NAMI organization. History and guides come with it.
  3. Jesse builds the shared record store for Volunteers & Events on the NAMI account (about a day), and moves Grant Radar and the Grant Dashboard across.
  4. Jesse turns on the sign-in in front of all three and adds each tester's email.
  5. Jesse updates every link and guide to the new address and sends a fresh set of emails. The old landing page points to the new one.
  6. Staff bring their phase-one records across with the backup files each program already makes.
  7. NAMI decides later whether to connect Microsoft sign-in; no change to the programs is needed for that.

What phase two makes possible

  • New programs slot in behind the same door and the same sign-in.
  • A program with private information, such as money, can be limited to named people.
  • A paid tier, if it is ever needed, is a small monthly fee on the NAMI account, not a rebuild.
The decision between the phases. After week 4, with the owners' answers in hand: has each program done one real job (an event run through it, a grant tracked to a report, a lead sent on)? If yes, phase two starts as soon as the internal person is confirmed. If not yet, phase one continues with fixes, and the question is asked again.

Who does what

The Executive DirectorForwards three emails, confirms the internal person, and makes the call between the phases.
Jesse BergBuilds and fixes the programs, runs the walkthroughs and check-ins, and moves everything in phase two.
One internal NAMI personCo-owns NAMI's accounts with Jesse, decides who gets access, and is the second person able to change the code.
One owner per programVolunteer coordinator: Volunteers & Events. Grants person: the Dashboard and Radar. Uses it, keeps the routine.

Later, when it is wanted

  • More programs. The landing page takes new cards, and every program follows the same rules: one name is enough, nothing is lost, every delete has Undo, plain words, a Help page.
  • Private programs. Money, or anything sensitive, gets the locked door from phase two and a short list of named people.
  • Connections. Email reminders, SharePoint documents and Microsoft sign-in are each a separate, later decision.
