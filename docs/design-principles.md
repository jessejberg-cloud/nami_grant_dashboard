# Design principles for the NAMI apps

Every NAMI app in this repository follows these rules: the Grant Dashboard, Grant Radar, and Volunteers & Events. They are written for staff with limited computer experience and for people with ADHD. Rules that work for them work for everyone.

1. **Lower the demand.** Only a name is ever required. Nothing forces a step, a field or a review before you can move on.
2. **No shame, no pressure.** Never count what wasn't done. No "overdue", no red, no streaks, no countdowns. A number may point forward ("2 spots open"), never back at a gap.
3. **Keep it calm.** Muted colors and one gentle fade between pages. Nothing flashes, buzzes or nags.
4. **Less on the screen.** Headings at a glance, details when you open something. Fewer words, bigger type. A few menu items, with the rest under More.
5. **One thing at a time.** Home shows the next thing to do. Short rows in place of long forms.
6. **Nothing is ever lost.** Everything saves as you type. A failed save keeps what was typed and says so plainly. A backup file is always one tap away.
7. **Everything can be undone.** A delete has Undo and goes to Recently deleted first. Nothing disappears on its own.
8. **It adapts to you.** Text size and light or dark colors are settings, and they're never locked.
9. **It explains itself.** One short welcome you can skip. Tips are one line and go away with "Got it". Help answers questions in plain words.
10. **Plain words.** Say what a person would say: event, volunteer, came, hours, gift. No system words like scope, record ID or aggregate.
11. **Setup is skippable, and the app already works.** Example records are only added when asked for, are labeled "Example", and come out in one tap.
12. **Keep private things out.** No health or diagnosis details, background-check reports, ID numbers or bank details. Notes hold only what planning and thank-yous need.
