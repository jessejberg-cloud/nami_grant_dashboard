# Leadership demonstration — 8–10 minutes

The existing Site is public. Use synthetic demonstration data only; screen-share for leadership if preferred. All sample awards, dollar amounts, requirements, staff labels and links are fictional. This is a working evaluation prototype, not authoritative compliance or accounting software.

Before presenting: choose Sample workspace; Settings → Reset demo data → confirm. This removes sample edits only; export first if you want to retain them. Select Overview → Start guided demo. Reset dates are relative to today.

1. **Needs Attention (1 minute).** “What needs my attention now?” Open the critical overspend issue. The queue ranks critical issues, overdue work and uncertainty with readable reasons. Counts do not certify that all award terms have been entered.
2. **Portfolio and details (1 minute).** Show the five award scenarios: simple community outreach, peer support, reporting-heavy family education, government aggregate outreach, and troubled recovery plan. Open the government award; show linked requirements, tasks, and issues.
3. **Requirements versus tasks (2 minutes).** Open Government monthly expenditure report. The report is a commitment; Finance staff: deliver aggregate expense schedule is its prerequisite task, already overdue. Attempt completion after adding a synthetic evidence URL: it must fail while the prerequisite is incomplete. Open Tasks, complete that task, return to the report, and complete it with a source link. A link is not independently verified evidence.
4. **Confidence and finance (1 minute).** Show STALE on the government award and UNKNOWN where no financial snapshot date exists. Show the troubled award’s negative remaining balance. Editing a general record does not refresh financial confidence. Accounting remains authoritative.
5. **Issues (1 minute).** Open the critical issue. Show severity, owner, escalation owner, deadline and status. Escalation is recorded; no message is sent.
6. **Radar (1 minute).** Open the fictional wellbeing opportunity; Promote to pending award. Funding remains unconfirmed. Repeating promotion is rejected. Weekly discovery is future agency configuration.
7. **History and handoff (1 minute).** Activity shows saved actions and changed field names. Settings exports records and accepts validated additive JSON imports. Show the future connection list. No Microsoft or accounting account is connected.

Close: “The agency can evaluate the workflow now. Production use requires its own identity, authoritative source connections, access rules, monitoring, and verified award terms.”
