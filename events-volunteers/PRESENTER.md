# Presenter Guide

Version 1.0.0 · October 5, 2026 · 8 to 10 minutes

Use the private Site while screen-sharing from the authorized account. Do not change sharing merely to demonstrate. The evaluation uses fictional names, contacts, sponsors, partners and events. It does not represent actual NAMI programs or policy.

1. Explain the audience: coordinators and Associate Director, with leadership review. For a roughly 30-person nonprofit, keep work practical; capacity can represent an occasional 500-person event without collecting attendee identities.
2. Complete/skip orientation; show Help reopening and the separate six-step guided demo. Load samples if empty; never reset someone else's work.
3. Open the home queues and explain unknown versus zero. Open Sample community welcome day, its staffing gap and blocked accessibility task.
4. Show Alex Sample's separate verification categories. Open Alex welcome assignment and explain Assigned versus Confirmed; confirmation records acceptance and sends nothing.
5. Show the historical three-hour shift and 150-minute submission. Demonstrate explicit approval; explain rejection/correction without duplicate counting.
6. Show Fictional Harbor Foods, the 50 boxed-lunch pledge and $500 corporate pledge. Keep food quantity and money separate. Show the fictional partner under discussion.
7. Show event capacity 500 and aggregate attendance fields, Preferences and Summaries. Explain that public attendee identity, payments and accounting are outside this prototype.
8. Export sample JSON; show import preview, conservative merge and explicit replacement. Show Word/PDF manual and quick start in Help.

Close with adoption boundaries: private per-evaluator records, no shared agency roles yet, no Microsoft 365 or outbound automation, and no automatic grant compliance or donation valuation. Use ADMIN_HANDOFF.md for agency preparation.
