# Nami Events and Volunteers Quick Start

Version 1.0.0 · October 5, 2026 · Evaluation with fictional data only

Coordinate small events and occasional events up to 500 people. This private prototype gives each signed-in evaluator separate durable records; it is not yet a shared agency system.

1. Open the app with the authorized ChatGPT account. Complete or skip onboarding. Help reopens it and offers a separate guided demonstration.
2. Select Fictional samples. On At a glance, choose Load fictional samples if empty. This never overwrites existing records.
3. Review upcoming events, unconfirmed shifts, overdue tasks, volunteer verification and hours awaiting review. Open a queue record. UNKNOWN means information failed to load or is missing; zero is not proof of complete coverage.
4. Events → Add event. Set the purpose, owner, location, dates, America/Chicago time zone, total capacity, logistics and next action. Add shifts and tasks from the event. Capacity counts all people; actual total attendance is an aggregate, separate from volunteers.
5. Volunteers → Add volunteer. Use fictional contact details. Review training, consent, screening and role approval separately. Verified needs a human date, verifier and non-sensitive basis.
6. Open a shift → Add assignment. Confirmed requires recorded acceptance. Overlaps, inactive volunteers and overfilled shifts are blocked. No invitations are sent.
7. Mark the assignment Attended, then Record attendance with actual times. Submit hours in minutes. Review explicitly. Reject approved hours before correcting and resubmitting; the current record is counted once.
8. Use Donors & sponsors and Partners for relationships. Add donation links money, food, goods or services to a contributor and optionally an event. Separate Pledged from Received; use in-kind quantities without dollar valuation.
9. Summaries shows actual volunteer counts and approved/pending hours. Reviewed aggregate exports do not establish grant compliance. Calendar downloads are manual snapshots, not synchronization.
10. Backup & exports → Download JSON backup before changes. Import preview explains merge and replace. Replace requires typing the scope confirmation. Preferences changes labels, categories, time zone, colors, layout and home order.

Help contains the full editable Word/PDF manual and administrator handoff. Failed saves retain input; for stale edits, preserve notes, close the form and Reload. No Microsoft 365 connections, email, notifications or background automation are active. Do not enter confidential or production data.
